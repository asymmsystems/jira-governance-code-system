feat(cascadeplan): page Jira reads, back off on 429 and apply cascades in batches

Issue search follows nextPageToken up to 10 pages of 100. When more
issues exist, the plan reports how many were shown and Jira's
approximate total, and the planner says so in the header. Every Jira
call retries a 429 after Retry-After, at most three times and within a
20 second budget.

updateWbsTask reads Jira once and builds the returned plan from the same
read. A cascade moves at most 25 successors per call and returns the
rest; the planner calls continueCascade until none remain.
previewCascade lists the moves a change would cause without writing.

Refs: B-10, CP-10, CP-17
