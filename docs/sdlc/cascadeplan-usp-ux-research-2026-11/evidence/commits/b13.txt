feat(cascadeplan): draw the timeline on a canvas with virtualised rows

The timeline area is one canvas, scaled by devicePixelRatio, that draws
the week grid, the bars with their progress, label chips, dependency
arrows with trigger labels, the today line and the drag preview. Colours
come from the --cp design tokens at draw time, so the Jira theme applies
and a theme change repaints. Every canvas colour pair reaches WCAG
contrast in light and dark mode, and a label sits on its own opaque chip
inside the bar or to the right of it.

Only the rows in view plus a buffer are DOM rows. Each visible bar has a
transparent, focusable hit element with an accessible name and 24 px
resize handles. Dragging snaps to days (Alt snaps to weeks) and previews
dependents live with the same schedule engine the backend uses, with
dashed outlines at their old positions. A drop sends one updateWbsTask
call with exact dates and the version, and the planner uses the plan it
returns without rebuilding rows. Drops from the backlog land on the week
under the pointer.

At 1,000 tasks the first render takes 140 ms and a hover 7 ms at most;
a drag costs 2.9 ms of script per frame at 1,000 and 5,000 tasks. A full
progress chip uses inverse text on the bold green fill.

Refs: B-13, NF-07
