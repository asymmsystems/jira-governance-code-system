feat(cascadeplan): licensing check in manifest and resolvers

Add app.licensing configuration with enabled true to manifest.yml, as
approved in scope review. In resolvers, evaluate context.license for active
state; when the installation is unlicensed (isActive or active is false),
return a licence required error result and perform zero writes to Jira or
KVS. Permitted contexts and environments without license enforcement continue
normal execution.

Refs: B-21
