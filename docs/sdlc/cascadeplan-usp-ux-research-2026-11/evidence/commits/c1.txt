feat(cascadeplan): multi-level WBS planner with inline editing and resource picker

The planner models Epic, Story/Task and Sub-task levels from the Jira
issue type hierarchy level and rolls up dates, effort and progress to
each parent, with a per-parent manual progress override.

Adds:
- Quick popovers for progress, target progress, status and epic link.
- Searchable team picker with lead and contributor roles.
- Column manager (hide, show, reset, resize) stored per project.
- Inline task creation below any row.
- Earliest-start week calculation from the predecessor's weekly
  allocation, used by the schedule cascade.
- WorkEffort, WorkEffortAssoc, WorkEffortPartyAssignment and
  WorkEffortEstimate types with WbsTask conversion functions.

Refs: CP-08, CP-14, CP-23
