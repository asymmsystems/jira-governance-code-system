feat(cascadeplan): estimation unit stored per task

Keep mixed estimation units (Hours, Story Points, T-Shirt) per task in the
WorkEffortEstimate extension without requiring data migration. When a task
is edited, changing the estimate updates the unit and raw estimate in KVS
and sends only the converted estimate in hours to Jira when hours actually
change. Reopening a task in the task detail modal restores the saved unit
and raw value, displaying the reconciled hours badge live.

Refs: B-20
