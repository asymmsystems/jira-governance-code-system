feat(cascadeplan): zoom levels, an open-ended timeline and frozen columns

The time axis is computed in the browser: one timeline column whose
width and header cells follow the zoom level, Day, Week, Month, Quarter
or Year, chosen from a toolbar or with Ctrl or Cmd and plus or minus.
The issue with grid focus stays in view when the zoom changes. The
range covers every scheduled date and grows by 26 weeks when the user
scrolls near either edge, without moving the view. Drops schedule an
exact start date, so they work anywhere on the timeline.

Drags snap to days at Day and Week zoom and to weeks above that; Alt
gives days at any zoom. The header row and the data columns stay in
place while the timeline scrolls, and a compact mode shows only Key and
Summary. Today, Earlier and Later move the view directly. Every line of
the hover tooltip uses inverse text on its dark background.
