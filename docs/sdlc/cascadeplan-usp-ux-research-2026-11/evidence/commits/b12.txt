feat(cascadeplan): show why each task starts when it does

Each dependent bar explains its start: the tooltip gives the engine's
reason, the connector shows the trigger percent and the trigger date,
and a "Why this date?" popup (row button or the ? key) lists the chain
of driving predecessors back to the first task with no constraint. Each
step focuses its row, and the driving bars get a dashed outline with a
"Driver" label. A violation states what to change and offers one button
per fix: move the task to its earliest start, or lower the trigger.

The engine is the only judge of a violation; the connector drawing no
longer adds its own test.

Refs: B-12, NF-03
