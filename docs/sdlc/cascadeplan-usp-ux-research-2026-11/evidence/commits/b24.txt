feat(cascadeplan): store planning data as Silverston WorkEffort records

Each issue's KVS record is a WorkEffort record: the stored WorkEffort
(dates, sequence, target and hand-set percent), its WorkEffortAssoc
precedence link (FRACTIONAL_MILESTONE with the trigger percent and the
Jira link id), WorkEffortPartyAssignment contributors and the
WorkEffortEstimate, plus the week-grid position. Jira-owned fields are
not part of the record. toWorkEffortRecord and fromWorkEffortRecord
convert between the record and the planner view without loss.

Refs: B-24
