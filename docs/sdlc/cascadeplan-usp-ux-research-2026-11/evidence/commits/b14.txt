feat(cascadeplan): undo every write with Flags instead of dialogs

Each schedule, unschedule and task update is recorded with its inverse:
the plan before the write is compared with the plan after it, and every
changed task, including the dependents the cascade moved, can be
restored and applied again. Twenty steps are kept. A parent whose dates
only follow a child is not restored separately, so an undo writes no
parent value to Jira. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z (Cmd on macOS) undo and
redo in the planner; text fields keep their own undo.

Each write shows a Flag with one Undo action that stays for 10 seconds
and while the pointer or focus is on it. A failed write shows an error
Flag that stays until it is closed. The planner opens no browser dialog.
A form with unsaved edits asks inside the form before Escape or a
backdrop click closes it.

Refs: B-14, CP-20, INV-12
