feat(cascadeplan): standup flow with multi-select and batch edits

Ctrl or Cmd+click toggles a row, Shift+click selects a range and Space
selects the focused row; Space on the Start or Due cell still moves the
bar. A selection bar sets progress, moves the selection by a number of
days or sets the assignee. In the schedule grid, digits set progress in
steps of ten and Enter saves every pending value.

Each batch is one batchUpdateTasks call with one Jira read: every item
runs like a single update, including its cascade, an item whose version
was changed by an earlier item of the same batch is retried, and a
version changed elsewhere is reported as a conflict. A batch is one
undo step.

Refs: B-16
