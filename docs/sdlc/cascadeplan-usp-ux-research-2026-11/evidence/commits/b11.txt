feat(cascadeplan): one date-based schedule engine with a stated reason

src/domain/schedule-engine.ts computes the earliest start of every
dependent task from WorkEffort efforts and FRACTIONAL_MILESTONE links,
in calendar days: planned basis (start plus the trigger share of the
predecessor's duration) or observed basis (the progress rate so far).
Each result names the driving predecessor and gives the reason, such as
"Starts 2026-11-13 because API-4 reaches 30% on 2026-11-12 (planned:
27 days)", and a violation says what to change.

The same engine drives the plan view, the move to the earliest start
when a dependency is set, the cascade (Kahn order, conflicting
successors only, durations kept), its continuation and its preview.
A dependency needs a trigger percent, and a plain Jira Blocks link
imports as finish-to-start. The team factor, capped at five people,
only suggests an end date.

Refs: B-11, CP-14, NF-03, NF-04
