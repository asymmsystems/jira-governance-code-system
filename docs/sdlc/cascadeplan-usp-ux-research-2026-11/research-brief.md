# CascadePlan: USP and UX research brief

- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Question:** why would a Jira team pick CascadePlan over Jira Plans and the main Marketplace planning apps, and what UX delivers that reason?
- **Code reviewed:** `apps/planshift` at commit `3ef4841` on branch `feature/cascadeplan-research-build` (the owner's work in progress, committed 2026-11-01). Every file:line reference points to `3ef4841`. Commit `0252680` adds one closing brace to `index.html` (NF-01), which moves later `index.html` lines down by 2.
- **Evidence grades** (audit scheme): Observed-code, Observed-test, Observed-live, Reported, Inferred. No live-site check ran in Part A.
- **Source IDs:** JP (Jira Plans), MP (Marketplace apps), RS (team size, Silverston, Forge limits), T (tests run in this research, see `round-log.md`). Section 8 lists every source.
- **Quote caveat:** web quotes came through a fetch tool that summarises page text. Check a quote against its URL before any external use.

## 1. Executive summary

1. One candidate comes close to a USP: a successor that starts when its predecessor reaches X%, with a computed start date and a stated reason (U1). No checked Jira tool offers it (JP-03, JP-07, MP-02, MP-16, MP-17, MP-31, MP-47). The owner approved U1, U2 and U3 as the direction on 2026-11-01.
2. Demand is real but modest: the matching Atlassian request has 56 votes in 10 years (JP-07), and "explain the schedule" closed for low engagement at 30 votes (JP-22).
3. U1 is not true in the product yet: the UI never shows the reason (T-04 T8), two engines disagree (T-05 Q2), and a dependency cannot be removed (T-05 Q4).
4. Release blockers CP-01, CP-02, CP-03, CP-04, CP-05 and CP-07 (modal path) are still open. The parse error (NF-01) is fixed in `0252680`.
5. Round 3 (UX): in 137 recent Cloud reviews of 7 rival apps, complexity and setup are the top complaints, and unwanted Jira writes are the most serious ones (UX-01 to UX-09). No rival documents keyboard scheduling, multi-step undo of bar drags, or a live preview of dependents (section 5; `ux-spec.md` 5.1).
6. Canvas stays (owner decision). A canvas prototype drew 5,000 tasks with drag and scroll frames at 16.8 ms or less, against 250 ms frames in the current DOM build at 1,000 tasks (T-03, T-06). It needs an accessible treegrid layer and its own contrast tests (`ux-spec.md` 5.4).
7. Order: fix the 7 blockers, make WorkEffort the real model with the KVS redesign, build one date-based engine with a visible reason, then canvas, undo, keyboard and first-run UX (`backlog.md`).
8. Do not market mixed estimation units, the heatmap or the team factor (section 4.2).
