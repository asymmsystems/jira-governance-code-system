# FourRealities: USP and UX research brief

- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Question:** why would a Jira team pay for FourRealities, compared with native Atlassian features and Marketplace apps, and what UX delivers that reason?
- **Code reviewed:** `apps/fourrealities` in the working tree of `gemini-earning` (branch `feature/cascadeplan-research-build`). The md5 of every FourRealities file equals the audit snapshot (T-00), so all file:line references match the audit.
- **Evidence grades** (audit scheme): Observed-live, Observed-code, Observed-test, Reported, Inferred. Unknown means searched and not verified.
- **Source IDs:** NA (native Atlassian), DD (DoD and checklist apps), AU (audit and approval apps), TR (traceability apps), EM (time in status and workflow apps), EX (external metrics tools), CM (composite metrics), BY (buyers and Runs on Atlassian), PR (pricing), EV (evidence, ADS, WCAG), RF (refute searches), FL (Forge limits), T (tests in this research, see `round-log.md`). Section 9 lists every source.
- **Quote caveat:** most web quotes came through a fetch tool that summarises page text. SPACE (CM-03), ISA 500 (EV-01, EV-02) and the Marketplace pricing JSON (PR-05 to PR-16) were checked against the primary text. Check any other quote against its URL before external use.

## 1. Executive summary

1. One candidate passes the USP test, with a condition: **one per-issue evidence view that names the source of each fact and states whether FourRealities verified it** (U1). No native feature or checked Marketplace app gives this for general software teams. Direct buyer demand is not shown (section 4).
2. The code does not deliver U1 today. The project page renders blank (FR-01: T-03, T-05), the remediations forge sibling evidence (FR-02, FR-03), and the Audit gate trusts any property (FR-04).
3. Review compliance per issue (U4) has a real gap and real demand (206 votes, NA-27). Forge has no generally available API for pull request approvals (NA-03 to NA-09, RF-03). The Code gate must say "heuristic" until that changes.
4. Rework loops (H4) and Confluence-link search (H2) are already sold: Timepiece (EM-01) and JQL Search Extensions (RF-04, RF-05). They are not USPs.
5. The RAI headline score carries the Goodhart and team-ranking risk that DORA and SPACE describe (H5, CM-01 to CM-03). Keep it, but show the gates first and the applicable-gate count.
6. Price: $1.50 is the top of the single-purpose band ($0.10 to $1.50), above Timepiece ($1.20) which already does the Process gate (H8).
7. Harness baseline: the history value passes 240 KiB on the 7th view at 50 issues, and on the 2nd view at 300 issues. After that every load fails (T-04).
8. Order of work: FR-01, delete the forging paths, honest gate states, storage and paging, then UX (`backlog.md`). Full trust needs signed LedgerSeal and DefinitionGuard properties, which is sibling-app work (audit section 5).
9. New site fact: a Jira Software project, AUDT, now exists. Sprint and epic checks can run there in Part B, with owner approval (T-05).

### 4.2 The three strongest candidates

1. **U1, one honest per-issue evidence view.** The only conditional pass. It is a product only after B-01 to B-21. It becomes strong after signed sibling properties (B-26).
2. **U4, review compliance per issue without egress.** Strongest gap and demand. Blocked by the platform (H11). Ship as "heuristic" now.
3. **U7, verified sibling attestations, widened to third-party DoD data.** A defensible moat if LedgerSeal and DefinitionGuard sign their properties and FourRealities also reads the HeroCoders `checklist` property (DD-21) as a labelled hint.
