When the migration assistant shows "This app is not available in cloud" for an approval, signature or checklist app, your client still needs the control. Our apps:

- **LedgerSeal** seals a Jira issue at approval, names every later change and who made it, and blocks the release transition until another approver approves the changes. It supports the 21 CFR 11.10(e) audit trail. It does not meet 11.200 or 11.300, so a client that needs Part 11 signatures keeps its own signing control.
- **DefinitionGuard** blocks the move to Done while a mandatory Definition of Done item is open, with evidence-required items, a not-the-assignee rule and waivers with reasons.
- **FourRealities** shows, per issue, whether a decision record, a Definition of Done, a pull request and an approval seal exist.
- **CascadePlan** is a timeline with percent-trigger dependencies and reviewed scope baselines.

All four run on Atlassian Forge, declare no external egress and keep their data in Atlassian storage. Isolated Cloud and Government Cloud listings are planned, not done.
