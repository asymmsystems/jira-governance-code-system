      How CascadePlan works
      • Your Jira issues are the plan. Drag an issue from the backlog onto the timeline to give it dates.
      • A percent link says when work can start: "B starts when A reaches 30%". Click a bar, then Add dependency.
      • When A slips, CascadePlan moves B and shows why. Every change to Jira is listed under Changes, and Ctrl+Z undoes it.
      • The keyboard works everywhere: Tab to the schedule, arrows to move, Space to move a bar, ? to see why a date is set.
